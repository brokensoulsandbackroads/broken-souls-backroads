What is Broken Souls & Backroads?

Not polished Nashville sunshine. Broken Souls & Backroads lives where country storytelling meets the bits of life people usually leave out of the photograph.

The songs move through family, grief, recovery, anxiety, love, bad decisions, second chances and the strange humour that sometimes turns up when everything else has gone sideways.

Some tracks whisper. Some kick the door in. They all come from the same place: real stories deserve a soundtrack too.
